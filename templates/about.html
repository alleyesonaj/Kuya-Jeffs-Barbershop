About Me

A craft built on dedication.

Established in 2014, Kuya Jeff’s Barbershop is a testament to dedication, craftsmanship, and the pursuit of excellence. What began as a humble venture has steadily evolved into a distinguished independent barbershop in San Pedro, Laguna, built through years of experience and a commitment to exceptional grooming.

At the heart of the business is Jeffrey Corvera, a professional barber originally from Bislig, Surigao del Sur, whose passion for the craft has shaped the identity of Kuya Jeff’s Barbershop. With a meticulous approach to every cut and a deep appreciation for traditional barbering, he continues to provide a refined and personalized experience for every client. From its modest beginnings to its continued success today, the barbershop remains a reflection of his dedication, character, and commitment to quality.
